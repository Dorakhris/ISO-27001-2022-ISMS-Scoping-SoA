# ISO 27001:2022 ISMS Scoping & Statement of Applicability 

## Overview

This project demonstrates how to conduct an **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System (ISMS) Scoping Workshop** and produce the initial documentation required before an organization begins its certification journey.

The scenario is based on a healthcare startup, **Safempire Health**, preparing to launch a cloud-based telemedicine platform that processes sensitive patient information across multiple jurisdictions.

Rather than focusing solely on compliance documentation, this project follows the thought process behind defining an ISMS, understanding the organization's business context, identifying critical assets, selecting applicable controls, and producing an implementation roadmap.



## Objectives

The workshop was designed to:

* Define the scope of the ISMS in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 4.3
* Identify internal and external stakeholders
* Document interested parties and their security requirements
* Identify critical business processes and information assets
* Establish ISMS boundaries and exclusions
* Produce an initial Statement of Applicability (SoA)
* Develop actionable recommendations for ISO 27001 implementation



## Scenario

Safempire Health is a telemedicine company preparing for commercial launch.

The platform processes Protected Health Information (PHI), integrates with Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems, and must satisfy multiple regulatory requirements, including:

* ISO/IEC 27001:2022
* HIPAA
* GDPR

The workshop focuses on designing an ISMS that supports business objectives while protecting sensitive healthcare data.

## Key Areas Covered

### Stakeholder Analysis

Identifying business stakeholders and understanding their security concerns before defining the ISMS.

### ISMS Scoping

Determining exactly which:

* Business processes
* Departments
* Information assets
* Cloud services
* Personnel
* Physical locations

should be included within the Information Security Management System.

### Statement of Applicability (SoA)

Preparing an initial SoA by evaluating selected Annex A controls and documenting:

* Applicability
* Justification
* Implementation approach
* Current maturity

### Implementation Planning

Developing practical actions required before certification, including governance, risk assessment, security awareness, incident response planning, and technical controls.

## Why This Project Matters

Many people associate ISO 27001 with policies and audits.

In reality, every successful ISMS begins with understanding the business.

Without clearly defining scope, identifying stakeholders, and understanding organizational context, security controls become difficult to justify, implement, and maintain.

This project demonstrates the foundational work that happens before risk assessments, policy development, and certification activities begin.
